## Group-level GSaE

The conclusion slide states that group-level GSaE was **not statistically significant (p = .075)**. The presentation therefore advises caution and humility when interpreting individual goalkeeper figures.

## Interpretation

The reported positive association between save percentage and GSaE suggests that the two shot-stopping measures move together in this sample. The reported relationship between xG faced and goals conceded is also positive. Goal-kick volume and average kick length show a positive association in the reported analysis.

The presentation does not support a claim that these correlations establish causation or that a single metric fully captures goalkeeper quality.

## Reporting caveat

The slide deck does not provide the complete underlying data, sample size for every test, confidence intervals, exact test statistics, or code. Do not treat this summary as an independently validated re-analysis.
